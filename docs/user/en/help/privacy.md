# Privacy summary

Zagens runs **locally** on your PC. This page summarizes product behavior; the legal text is on [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## What stays on your machine

- API keys and `config.toml` under your user profile
- Chat threads, workspace files, symbol index cache
- Usage statistics shown in the [usage dashboard](/docs/settings/usage)
- Office deliverables in your chosen workspace folders

## What leaves your machine

- **LLM requests** to the provider you configure (DeepSeek, NIM, OpenRouter, self-hosted, …)
- **Web tools** when enabled — URLs and search queries per [network policy](/docs/settings/network)
- **Vision** images when you use `describe_image`
- **In-app updates** — version check to `zagens.com` (no chat content)

## What we do not do

- No Zagens cloud account required for core chat
- No uploading your repo to zagens.com by default

## Your controls

- Disable web search and shell in [execution policy](/docs/settings/exec-policy)
- Use read-only sandbox for untrusted workspaces
- Pick providers and regions via [API settings](/docs/settings/api-key)
