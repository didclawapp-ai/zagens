# Contract draft

**Skill:** `office-contract-draft` · **Output:** DOCX (template draft)

## What it does

Drafts a first-pass contract or agreement DOCX from your requirements — parties, scope, payment, liability, dispute resolution.

> **Not legal advice.** Output is a starting template; have qualified counsel review before signing.

## Before you start

- Task type: **Office**
- Optional: existing template or clause list in `inbox/`
- Skill may **confirm before render** (`confirm_before_render: true`)

## How to run

1. Tap **Contract draft** or ask:
   > Draft a service agreement between Party A and Party B. Confirm contract type and jurisdiction.
2. Provide: contract type, parties, subject matter, term, governing law / venue.
3. Review the draft in `deliverables/`; iterate with follow-up edits in chat.

## Sections (typical)

Parties · subject matter · rights & obligations · payment · breach · dispute resolution · signature page

## Verify

- Party names and dates match your inputs
- Disclaimer about legal review is acknowledged in chat
