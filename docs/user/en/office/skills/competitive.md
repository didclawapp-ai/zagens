# Competitive analysis

**Skill:** `office-competitive-analysis` · **Output:** DOCX with cited sources

## What it does

Researches competitors or market moves on the web, compares findings, and delivers a structured brief with a **source list** — not a chat-only summary.

## Before you start

- Task type: **Office**
- Network enabled for `web_search` / `fetch_url`
- Optional notes in `inbox/`

## How to run

1. Tap **Competitive analysis** on the Office empty state, or ask:
   > Research recent moves by competitors A and B. Deliver a DOCX with sources.
2. Answer clarifying questions (targets, time range, comparison dimensions).
3. Find the DOCX under `deliverables/` when the turn completes.

## Output sections (typical)

Summary · competitor comparison · strengths/weaknesses · recommendations · **sources** (title, URL, date)

## Verify

- Source list is present; URLs look real
- No fabricated pricing or dates
