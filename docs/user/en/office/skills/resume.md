# Resume & cover letter

**Skill:** `office-resume` · **Output:** DOCX

## What it does

Writes or refreshes a resume (and optional cover letter) tailored to a target role, using your background and an optional job description.

## Before you start

- Task type: **Office**
- Optional: old resume or JD in `inbox/` (DOCX, PDF)
- Decide language (English / Chinese) and whether you need a cover letter

## How to run

1. Tap **Resume / cover letter** or ask:
   > Prepare a resume for a senior product manager role. Include a cover letter.
2. Provide: target role, company (optional), highlights, years of experience, skills.
3. DOCX in `deliverables/` — cover letter may be a section or separate file per your request.

## Sections (typical)

Contact · summary · **experience** (reverse chronological) · skills · education · cover letter

## Verify

- Employment dates and titles match your facts
- No invented employers or degrees

## Tips

- Paste the JD text in chat for better keyword alignment.
- Ask for a one-page version or ATS-friendly plain layout.
