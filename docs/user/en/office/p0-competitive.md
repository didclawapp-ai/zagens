# P0-1: Competitive / market watch

**Skill:** `office-competitive-analysis` · **Output:** DOCX with cited sources

## What it does

Researches competitors or market moves on the web, compares findings, and delivers a structured brief — not a chat summary.

## Before you start

- Task type: **Office**
- Optional: drop reference notes into `inbox/`
- Network access for `web_search` / `fetch_url`

## How to run

1. Open Zagens, pick **Office**, select your workspace.
2. On the empty state, tap **Competitive analysis** — or paste a goal like:
   > Research recent moves by competitors A and B in the EU market. Deliver a DOCX with sources.
3. Answer clarifying questions (targets, time range, comparison dimensions).
4. Find the DOCX under `deliverables/` when the turn completes.

## Output sections (typical)

Summary · competitor comparison · strengths/weaknesses · recommendations · **sources** (title, URL, date)

## Verify

- Source list is present and URLs look real
- No fabricated pricing or dates
